Tap sounds in the room. A two-octave classroom piano and 57 more music tools for beat, rhythm, singing, listening, movement and classroom routines. Use it on a projector or a tablet.

Pick a tool. One opens at a time. ⛶ Big screen fills the projector. Sound starts only when you tap, and every sound is made on this device, so the tools keep working offline once the page has loaded. Share a tool with a link ending in its name, for example #piano.
